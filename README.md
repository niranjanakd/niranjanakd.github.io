# Introvert Club 🌿

## A Comfortable Space for Introverts

Introvert Club is a community-focused website designed to create a comfortable digital space for introverts.

The platform provides a collection of fun and practical tools to help users handle common social situations, explore their comfort zones, and connect with a supportive community — without unnecessary pressure.

It also features **Mayavi**, an AI chatbot designed to act as a friendly digital companion and provide conversational support.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌱 Community Space
- A welcoming digital space for introverts
- Encourages connection and sharing experiences
- Designed around comfort, individuality, and personal space

### 🤖 Mayavi – AI Chatbot
Mayavi is an AI-powered chatbot available on the platform.

It acts as a friendly digital companion that users can interact with while navigating different social situations.

### 🧰 Interactive Social Tools

The website includes several fun tools designed around everyday social situations:

- 🚨 **Small Talk Escape Route**
  - Generate excuses to leave awkward conversations

- 🔋 **Conversation Energy Meter**
  - Check your social energy before interacting

- 🐧 **Awkward Silence Filler**
  - Generate conversation fillers for uncomfortable silences

- 📱 **Social Event Excuse Generator**
  - Generate excuses for avoiding or leaving social events

- 📱 **Text Regret Predictor**
  - Explore how much you might regret sending a message

- 🧠 **Did I Sound Weird Analyzer**
  - Analyze conversations and get playful feedback

- 🛒 **Grocery Human Avoidance**
  - Plan routes designed around avoiding unnecessary interactions

- ☎️ **Phone Call Simulator**
  - Practice or simulate phone conversations

- 💬 **Group Chat Participation Optimizer**
  - Find suitable moments to join group conversations

- 🎉 **Party Attendance Guilt Calculator**
  - Calculate your "guilt" about skipping social events

- 🌦️ **Introvert Weather App**
  - A playful weather experience designed for staying indoors

## 🎯 Vision

We envision a world where introverts can maintain their comfort zones while navigating everyday social situations with greater confidence.

Introvert Club aims to combine **community, technology, and humor** to create a space where users can be themselves without feeling pressured to constantly socialize.

---

## 💡 Why Introvert Club?

Social situations can sometimes feel overwhelming.

Instead of forcing users to become more social, Introvert Club focuses on providing:

- Comfort
- Humor
- Practical tools
- Digital companionship
- Community
- Personal space

The goal is to make everyday social experiences feel a little easier and more enjoyable.
